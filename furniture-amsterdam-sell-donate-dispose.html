What to do with your furniture when you leave Amsterdam

By Olav and Rosalie Gubbels · Updated 18 October 2026 · 3 min read

In short

  • Start six weeks before you leave. Furniture sells slowly when you are in a hurry.
  • Most used furniture has little resale value. Sell the few good pieces, give the rest away.
  • Charities often do not collect. Call before you plan on it.
  • Amsterdam is changing how bulky waste works. Check the current rules before you book anything.

Sort first

Walk through the apartment with a list and put each item in one of four groups: coming with you, selling, giving away, throwing out. Be strict. Anything you are unsure about after one minute goes in the second or third group. You can always keep a few things back at the end.

Selling

  • Use Marktplaats, Facebook Marketplace and the Facebook groups for internationals in Amsterdam. Pick the platform where people collect items from you.
  • Write the dimensions in the advert, add good photos in daylight and say clearly when it must be collected.
  • Price for a quick sale. Most used furniture sells for far less than people expect. Items from design brands or in very good condition are the exception.
  • Sell large items early and keep the small stuff for later. Set a date after which unsold items are given away.

Giving away

Colleagues, neighbours and the same Facebook groups are often the fastest route. Put "free, collect by Friday" in the title. Someone will come.

Charity second-hand shops (kringloopwinkels) can take good furniture, but many have limits on what they accept and when they can collect. Phone them first. Do not leave anything outside a shop.

Disposing of what is left

Do not put furniture on the street. Dumping in public space can be fined, and neighbours and landlords notice. In Amsterdam, bulky waste (grofvuil) collection is being redesigned. In 2025 the city announced that, from 2026, residents would mostly bring bulky waste to recycling points at the edge of the city or book a collection by appointment. See the report by NH Nieuws. Rules and costs can differ by district and may have changed again, so look at the current page on amsterdam.nl before you plan.

When you are short on time or abroad

This is what we do every week. We go through the apartment with you, look at what can be donated or reused, and remove the rest. If something is clearly still worth money, we offer you a fair price for it. Because we look at reuse first, less ends up in the container.

Most furniture has little resale value, so please do not count on it to pay for the move. If we make an offer, we tell you before you decide anything.

A simple timeline

  1. Six weeks before: sort and take photos of what you want to sell.
  2. Four weeks before: publish the adverts. Choose a date for the leftovers.
  3. Two weeks before: give away what has not sold. Ask for a quote for the rest.
  4. Last week: clearance, clean and photos.
