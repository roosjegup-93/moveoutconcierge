What drives the cost of an apartment clearance in Amsterdam

By Olav and Rosalie Gubbels · Updated 18 October 2026 · 3 min read

In short

  • The price depends on volume, floors and stairs, access, disposal and extras like cleaning.
  • A fair quote is based on photos or a visit, not a guess over the phone.
  • Ask what is included and what happens if something changes.
  • Clear the small things yourself to bring the price down.

We do not publish a price list, because two apartments of the same size can be a very different job. What we can do is explain what moves the price, so you can compare quotes properly.

What sets the price

  • Volume. How much has to leave. A studio with a bed and a table is one job. A family flat with a full storage room is another.
  • Floors and stairs. A fourth floor with a steep staircase takes more people and time than a ground floor. Whether there is a lift matters as well.
  • Access. Where the van can stop, whether a parking permit or a reserved space is needed, and whether the building restricts moving times.
  • Large or heavy items. Pianos, safes, solid wardrobes and anything that has to be taken apart.
  • Disposal. What goes to recycling, what is reused and what is waste. Some materials cost more to dispose of.
  • Extras. Cleaning, repairs and handover help are separate from the clearance itself.
  • Timing. The last days of a month are usually the busiest, since many leases end then. Booking early gives you more choice.

How to get a quote you can trust

  1. Send photos of every room, the storage room and the entrance and stairs.
  2. Say what you want done: clearance only, or cleaning and repairs as well.
  3. Ask for the price in writing, and what it includes.
  4. Ask what happens if there is more than expected. With us, we message you first before anything extra.

Questions worth asking any provider

  • Is the price fixed once it is agreed?
  • Does it include disposal fees and parking?
  • Do you take care of the handover instructions from the landlord?
  • Do I get photos after the work?
  • Can you work in English if I am abroad?

How to lower the price

  • Sell or give away the larger, good pieces yourself. Our guide: what to do with your furniture.
  • Take the clothes, kitchenware and small items to a charity yourself.
  • Put things you want to keep in one room so they are not mixed up.
