Moving out can feel like a second job.

Maybe your contract here has ended, maybe you're going home, or your company is sending you somewhere new. Either way, the apartment has to be handed back, and there's a lot in it.

There's the rental contract and the final inspection. There's furniture that won't fit in a suitcase, things to sell, give away or throw out, a cleaning, some small repairs and a set of keys. And usually a date that can't move.

We take all of that off your plate. One message to us instead of five calls to five different companies.

Common questions

Do I need to be in Amsterdam for the move-out?

No. If we've agreed what needs doing and we have access to the apartment, we can do the work while you're abroad.

Can you buy my furniture?

Only when something is clearly still worth money. Most furniture has little resale value, so please don't count on it. If we do make an offer, we tell you before you decide anything.

How much does furniture removal or apartment clearance cost in Amsterdam?

It depends on how much there is, which floor you live on, whether there's a lift and what else you need, like cleaning or repairs. Send a few photos and we'll give you a quote.

Will I get my rental deposit back?

We can't promise it, because your landlord or property manager makes the final call. But we work from your handover instructions and know exactly what the final inspection looks for, so our handovers are normally approved without problems.

Can you end my rental contract for me?

No, that has to be done by you or by someone you authorise. We can give you a checklist, help you word the message and keep track of the dates.

Which areas do you work in?

Amsterdam and the surrounding region.
