Clearing a rental after a tenant leaves: a guide for landlords and agents

By Olav and Rosalie Gubbels · Updated 18 October 2026 · 3 min read

In short

  • Photograph everything before you touch it.
  • Put your request to the tenant in writing, with a deadline.
  • Separate clearing, repairs and cleaning, and do them in that order.
  • A single contact who communicates in English saves time with international tenants.

Many Amsterdam rentals are let to international tenants who move out on a fixed date, sometimes in a hurry. If you are a landlord, property manager or agent, the days between the old tenant leaving and the new one arriving are short. This guide covers how to use them well.

Before the tenant leaves

  • Send handover instructions early and in English if your tenant is international.
  • Offer a pre-inspection a few weeks before the end date.
  • Agree who takes over any items the tenant wants to leave, such as flooring or kitchen appliances. Write it down.

When things are left behind

It happens: furniture, boxes or rubbish are still in the home after the keys are returned. Do not start throwing things away. First:

  1. Photograph the home and the items as you found them.
  2. Contact the tenant in writing and ask what should happen to the items. Give a reasonable deadline.
  3. Check your contract and, where the value is significant or the tenant objects, get legal advice before disposing of anything.

The rules around belongings left behind can be more complicated than they look, and this page is not legal advice.

Clearing, repairs and cleaning in the right order

  1. Clearing. Everything out, including the storage room and balcony.
  2. Repairs. Filling holes, small paint touch-ups, fixing fittings.
  3. Cleaning. Last, so the next tenant arrives at a clean home and not a dusty one.

Having one party handle all three means fewer appointments and a shorter gap between tenants.

Reporting

A before-and-after set of photos per room, plus a short note on what was done, is what most landlords and agents need. It supports your handover report and your discussion about the deposit if the tenant questions a deduction. Our guide on deposit rules covers the deadlines that apply to you.
